{appName} shows you one task at a time, full screen, as soon as you open the app. Instead of a long list that overwhelms you, you only see what is next; when you complete it, the following one appears.

What you can do
- Write a task and choose whether it becomes the current one or goes to the end of the queue.
- Attach a photo (taken with the system camera or picked from your gallery), several photos (up to 10, which you swipe through in a carousel), a PDF or a web page, and see it full screen.
- Complete it by pressing and holding a button: the note tears and you move on to the next one. Completing a task deletes it completely, with no history or trash.
- Delete a task and undo it for a few seconds.
- See all your tasks and change their order.

Privacy
- No accounts, no server and no analytics, and no ads. Your tasks are kept on your phone.
- It doesn't need camera, photo or file permissions: it uses the system camera and picker, and only receives what you choose.
- The web task needs a connection: the page loads live and isn't saved. Creating, viewing, completing and deleting tasks works offline.
- Notifications are optional and come turned off. If you turn them on, your phone shows 1 to 3 notices a day with your current task; on Android 13 or later, the system asks for permission at that moment. With the phone locked, your system decides whether the notification shows in full.

Accessibility
- Designed to work with TalkBack, keyboard or Switch Access: almost every gesture has an alternative action.
- Follows Android's text size and the remove animations option.

Languages
- Available in Spanish and English; you change language in Settings, with no restart.
